01 About

Security is a process. So is becoming good at it.

I'm a Cyber Defense student in my final semester (Estácio, GPA 9.87/10), with a Bachelor's in Mechanical Engineering from ULBRA. The engineering background taught me to think in systems and failure modes — a mindset I now apply to security.

Focused on SOC operations: log analysis, alert triage, threat hunting with Splunk, and incident response. I document every lab on GitHub so the reasoning is visible, not just the result.

Outside security, I do visual work — cover art and typography. It's a hobby, not a service. Just the other half of the same curiosity: paying attention to how things are made.
